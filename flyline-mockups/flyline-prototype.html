Common questions

What time should I leave for Bhogapuram Airport from Madhurawada?
From Madhurawada, allow 70–80 minutes via NH-16. Add your airline's check-in cutoff (45 min IndiGo, 60 min Air India) and ~35 min terminal processing. For a 6:15am flight, leave by 4:10am.
How far is Bhogapuram Airport from Visakhapatnam?
Bhogapuram Airport (VTZ) is about 42 km from Vizag city centre via NH-16. Drive time ranges from 65 minutes off-peak to 85 minutes at morning peak.
What is IndiGo's check-in cutoff at Bhogapuram?
IndiGo closes check-in 45 minutes before departure for domestic flights. SpiceJet is 45 minutes; Air India is 60 minutes. FlyLine applies your airline's exact cutoff automatically.
